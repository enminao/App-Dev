# Breaking Bad

*Breaking Bad* is an American crime drama created by Vince Gilligan. It ran on AMC from January 20, 2008 to September 29, 2013, for five seasons and 62 episodes. The story takes place in Albuquerque, New Mexico.

> Spoiler warning: the character notes and season summaries below give away major plot points, including the ending.

## Premise

Walter White is a 50-year-old high school chemistry teacher who is underpaid, overqualified, and working a second job at a car wash. After a diagnosis of inoperable lung cancer, he worries about what will happen to his family when he dies. He decides to cook methamphetamine, using his chemistry knowledge to make a product purer than anything else on the market. Gilligan has described the show as the story of Mr. Chips becoming Scarface.[^1]

## Season summaries

1. Season 1 (2008): Walt teams up with a former student, Jesse Pinkman, and they cook in an RV in the desert. Their first dealings with the local drug trade turn violent.
2. Season 2 (2009): Walt and Jesse look for a way to move their product. They meet a lawyer named Saul Goodman, and Walt's lies begin to strain his marriage.
3. Season 3 (2010): Skyler learns the truth about Walt's work. Hank closes in on the person he knows only as Heisenberg.
4. Season 4 (2011): Walt and the drug lord Gus Fring turn against each other, and the conflict ends with one of them dead.
5. Season 5 (2012 to 2013): Walt builds his own operation and becomes the most dangerous person in it. The final eight episodes follow the fallout when Hank finds out who Heisenberg is.

### Walter White

Walt starts as a quiet man who feels overlooked, and cancer gives him the push to take control. He first says he is cooking for his family's financial security. Over time it becomes clear that he also likes being good at it and being feared. By the end he admits that he did it for himself.

### Jesse Pinkman

Jesse is a small-time dealer and a poor student who ends up as Walt's partner. He is the character most affected by the violence around him, and he carries a lot of guilt for it. Many fans see his story as the emotional center of the series.

### Skyler White

Skyler is smart and observant, and she notices Walt's lies long before he admits to anything. After she learns the truth, she is caught between protecting her children and helping a man she no longer trusts. Her role has been debated a lot among viewers, and Anna Gunn wrote about the hostility she received for the part.

### Hank Schrader

Hank is a loud, confident DEA agent who spends much of the series hunting Heisenberg without knowing it is his own family. He is shaken by a violent attack in season 3 and has to rebuild himself. His fight to recover shows more of him than his early jokes did.

### Saul Goodman

Saul is a lawyer who advertises on late-night television and has contacts for every problem. He is a source of dark humor, and he is also a useful guide to how the criminal world in Albuquerque works.

### Gus Fring

Gus is the calm, careful owner of the Los Pollos Hermanos chicken chain. He uses it as cover for a large drug distribution network. He is Walt's most capable opponent, and what makes him dangerous is his patience.

### Mike Ehrmantraut

Mike is a former police officer who works as Gus's fixer. He is gruff and practical, and he has little tolerance for sloppy mistakes. He often says what the audience is thinking about Walt's decisions.

## After the show

- *Better Call Saul* (2015 to 2022) is a prequel that follows Jimmy McGill, who becomes Saul Goodman. Gus and Mike are major characters in it.
- *El Camino: A Breaking Bad Movie* (2019) follows Jesse in the hours after the series finale.

The series finale is titled "Felina." It was widely praised, and *Breaking Bad* is often listed among the best television dramas ever made.

For episode guides and cast details, see the [Breaking Bad page on Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Breaking_Bad).
